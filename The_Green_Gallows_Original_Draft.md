## Cold Open — The Green Gallows, 400 Years Ago

A snow-covered field. Silent. Then — a mechanical screaming that vibrates in our teeth.

BODIES. Thousands. Frozen in mud. A green aurora flickers above, casting sick light on the dead.

A FIGURE in aristocratic velvet walks among them. He stops at a mother clutching a child. Both frozen.

He does not weep. He presses a RING into hot wax. The crest: a Wolf.

Snow falls on the dead. The Green Gallows has been opened.

**TITLE CARD: THE GREEN GALLOWS**

> **Purpose:** Establish the world, the family, the brothers. Make the audience invest in Jamie before we kill him.

## Scene 1 — Morning at Harding Manor

*ACT I — "THE HOUSE OF HARDING"*

**STRIKING LOCATION:** Harding Manor. Morning light through tall windows. Green-tinted lamps on every surface.

NATHANIEL HARDING (early 20s, rumpled, half-dressed) stumbles through the corridor. He slept badly. The nightmares again. He presses a white handkerchief to his nose. Faint blood.

He finds JAMIE HARDING (late 20s, impeccably dressed, already working) in the study, surrounded by open ledgers and power-grid schematics.

> **NATHANIEL:** "You look like you've been up all night."
>
> **JAMIE:** (closing a ledger) "Someone has to keep the lights on."
>
> **NATHANIEL:** "That's what Father says."
>
> **JAMIE:** (beat, something behind his eyes) "Yes. It is."
>
> **NATHANIEL:** "Then let me hold one end of it. Take me to the grid works this week."

Jamie does not answer. He turns his signet ring, wolf crest facing inward, hiding it.

**What the audience sees:** Two brothers. One carries the weight. One doesn't know there's weight to carry.

> ✏ **EP 1 PASS (mine):** plays as one continuous morning with Scene 2 (study, then the breakfast table). Jamie's signals before the party are cut to the ring here and the water glass at breakfast, so the walk and the farewell are the ones that count.

## Scene 1B — THE UNDERCITY: "The Job"

*ACT I — "THE HOUSE OF HARDING"*

> **INTERCUT NOTE:** From here, Episode 1 runs two stories in parallel — the Hardings above, the sisters below. They never meet. They are the same story.

**STRIKING LOCATION:** The Narrows. A rain-slicked courtyard behind a merchant's house with no sign and too many guards.

Two figures cross the roofline without a sound.

**MIRIAM BOUNDS** (23) — patient, watchful, the one with the plan. **ALICE BOUNDS** (21) — coiled, silent, the one with the knife.

Vykra colours under their coats. A contract: a house that has been selling people, and a patron who wants it closed.

> **ALICE:** "Twelve guards. Four on the door, two on the stair."
>
> **MIRIAM:** "And?"
>
> **ALICE:** "And nothing. I'm counting so you don't have to."
>
> **MIRIAM:** (a small smile) "You always count. You never wait."
>
> **MIRIAM:** "Fast. Quiet. Home before the bells."
>
> **ALICE:** "You have somewhere to be."
>
> **MIRIAM:** (not answering) "Fast. Quiet."

**Plant:** Miriam has somewhere to be. Alice notices. Alice always notices.

## Scene 2 — The Family Breakfast

*ACT I — "THE HOUSE OF HARDING"*

**STRIKING LOCATION:** The Harding breakfast table. Silver. Crystal. Green-powered warming plates.

ELDGARD HARDING (50s, iron patriarch) presides. JOSEPHINE HARDING (50s, steel matriarch) sits opposite.

The conversation is about tonight's party. Eldgard rehearses talking points. Josephine corrects his phrasing with surgical precision.

Jamie eats little. When Eldgard says "The grid will hold for another century," Jamie reaches for his water.

BYRON BLACKWOOD (60s, butler, scarred hands) places toast before Nathaniel. His knuckles — old knife scars — catch the light.

> **NATHANIEL:** "Byron, you've set an extra place."
>
> **BYRON:** "Lord Lambrick confirmed attendance this morning, sir."

The table goes quiet. Eldgard and Josephine exchange a look. Jamie's jaw tightens.

> **ELDGARD:** (carefully neutral) "Lord Lambrick is always welcome."
>
> **JOSEPHINE:** (to no one) "Lord Lambrick is never welcome. He is simply unavoidable."

Nathaniel tries again, to the whole table this time, with the nerve of a man who has rehearsed it.

> **NATHANIEL:** "Father. I'd like to learn the grid. Properly. Jamie can't carry all of it."
>
> **ELDGARD:** "Tonight you will stand in a room and let people look at you. That is the work."
>
> **JOSEPHINE:** (not unkind) "Your brother has the grid. Yours is the harder job: to be someone worth protecting."

Nathaniel looks down at his toast. Jamie looks at nothing. Byron refills the coffee.

> ✏ **EP 1 PASS (mine):** this gives Nathaniel a want in the first ten minutes (a place in the family work) and a refusal. The Fairview letter in Scene 5B then answers it in the cruellest possible way: his father gives him a way out and no seat at the table.

**What the audience learns:** The Hardings are powerful but besieged. Lambrick is a threat. The parents lie fluently. Jamie carries something unsaid. Byron sees everything.

## Scene 2B — THE UNDERCITY: "The Cargo"

*ACT I — "THE HOUSE OF HARDING"*

**STRIKING LOCATION:** A lamplit storeroom. Crates that are not crates. And a line of girls, roped at the wrist.

**THE KINGPIN** — a soft, well-dressed man the Lanes call **KESTREL** — is selling.

**AJAX FINK** is buying. Pleasant face. Cold eyes. He counts heads the way a man counts coin.

> **AJAX:** "Twelve."
>
> **KESTREL:** "Fourteen. Two are for the house on Pell Street."
>
> **AJAX:** "Then twelve reach the brothels and two don't reach anywhere. I'll pay for twelve."
>
> **KESTREL:** "You'll pay for fourteen. The paperwork says fourteen."

Ajax smiles. It is worse than shouting.

> **AJAX:** "The paperwork."
>
> **KESTREL:** "...Fourteen."

High above, through a grate: Miriam's face. She has stopped counting exits.

> **ALICE:** (whisper) "Miriam. We're here for the ledger."
>
> **MIRIAM:** "I know what we're here for."

**What the audience feels:** The party upstairs and the cargo downstairs are the same economy. The men dancing at Harding Manor are the men on Kestrel's ledger.

## Scene 3 — The Brothers Walk

*ACT I — "THE HOUSE OF HARDING"*

**STRIKING LOCATION:** Harding estate grounds. Late morning. Green-powered garden lights dot the path.

This is the scene that earns Jamie's death. The audience must LIKE him.

> **NATHANIEL:** "You've been different lately. Quiet."
>
> **JAMIE:** "I've always been quiet. You're just loud enough for both of us."
>
> **NATHANIEL:** "I'm serious. You look like Father when he's calculating something."

Jamie stops walking. Looks at his brother — really looks.

> **JAMIE:** "If something happened to me — if I couldn't be here anymore — what would you do?"
>
> **NATHANIEL:** (laughing) "Sell the house. Move to the countryside. Raise dogs."
>
> **JAMIE:** (not laughing) "I mean it."

Nathaniel's smile fades.

> **NATHANIEL:** "Why are you asking me this?"
>
> **JAMIE:** "Because tonight, Father is going to put a crown on my head. And I need to know that if I take it off... someone else can carry it."

Long beat.

> **NATHANIEL:** "You're the heir. You've always been the heir. I'm the spare. That's how this works."
>
> **JAMIE:** (quiet, sad) "Maybe it shouldn't."

He claps Nathaniel's shoulder. Walks ahead.

**What the audience feels:** Dread. Jamie is saying goodbye. The audience knows it. Nathaniel doesn't.

## Scene 3B — THE UNDERCITY: "The Takedown"

*ACT I — "THE HOUSE OF HARDING"*

Fast. Quiet. Six seconds per man.

Miriam takes the stair guards — efficient, almost bored — while Alice goes through the room like weather. A blade, a wrist, a body. No wasted motion, no wasted breath.

**THE KINGPIN:** Kestrel reaches for a bell. Miriam puts a hand flat on the table.

> **MIRIAM:** "The ledger."
>
> **KESTREL:** "Do you know who buys from me? Do you know what they'll —"
>
> **MIRIAM:** "I know exactly what they are. That's why I'm here."

She dispatches him with the same economy she uses for everything. **It is not a fight. It is punctuation.**

**AJAX GETS AWAY.** He is already through the back with the ledger's first pages, unhurried, unafraid — and at the last moment he turns and **looks directly at Miriam.** Files her face. Smiles.

> **AJAX:** "Tell your patron the house is closed."

He is gone.

**Plant:** Ajax has seen Miriam's face. That is a debt the season collects in Episode 6.

> **Purpose:** Convergence. Introduce the political players. Build tension. The Red Flower scene is the centerpiece.

## Scene 4 — The Grand Hall

*ACT II — "THE PARTY"*

**STRIKING LOCATION:** Harding Manor transformed. Chandeliers blazing green. Two hundred guests — all the royalty, high court, and aristocratic establishment are in attendance, raising the political stakes. An orchestra.

Quick establishing beats:

- **NIXON HARGRAVE** (25, dress uniform, medals) arrives with **TAMARA LAMBRICK** on his arm. She catalogues the room. Nixon quietly notes her father is watching them; Tamara coldly replies that Lambrick watches everyone, noting she is merely disposable insurance compared to her brother Edmund.

- **LORD LAMBRICK** (50s, predator in formal dress) enters. A RED FLOWER in his lapel — the only color in a sea of black and white.

- **AJAX FINK** arrives last. Night air still on his coat, a smear of ledger ink on one cuff. He crosses the hall unhurried, and behind a column he passes a folded packet to Lambrick, who takes it without looking at him. Pleasant face. Cold eyes scanning every guest.

> ✏ **EP 1 PASS (mine):** this answers how Ajax can be at the storeroom and at the party in one evening. The packet is the ledger's first pages, so Miriam's "Lambrick's man" in Scene 4B is now something the audience has seen.

- **BYRON** circulates among staff. His hand brushes the concealed blade at his hip. Old habit.

## Scene 4B — THE UNDERCITY: "The Report"

*ACT II — "THE PARTY"*

**STRIKING LOCATION:** A back room. No crest. No name. A **VYKRA HEAD** — a woman with a clerk's hands — counts coin onto the table.

A fat purse. Heavier than the job.

> **VYKRA HEAD:** "The house is closed. The girls are placed. The patron is satisfied."
>
> **MIRIAM:** "The patron should know a man walked out with the first pages. Lambrick's man."
>
> **VYKRA HEAD:** (a pause, then, evenly) "Then the ledger isn't finished. There will be other nights."

**Plant:** Vykra protects the Lanes' children and closes houses that sell them. That is how they recruit — and it is not why they were founded. Both of those things are true.

## Scene 5 — Eldgard's Announcement

*ACT II — "THE PARTY"*

Eldgard takes the grand staircase. Jamie stands beside him — the presented heir.

Eldgard addresses the room like a seasoned crisis politician — publicly acknowledging the recent disruptions and frequent blackouts affecting thousands across London, before smoothly reassuring the assembled elite that the Harding Power Grid's future is secured for another century to come.

Applause. Champagne raised. The political crowd is pacified. Jamie applauds with the crowd, but his eyes show he knows the promise is a lie.

## Scene 5B — HARDING MANOR: "The Letter"

*ACT II — "THE PARTY"*

**STRIKING LOCATION:** The grand hall, mid-party. Chandeliers blazing green.

Eldgard steers Nathaniel through the crowd toward a thin man in academic grey: **PROFESSOR WREN**, of the **Fairview Institute of Science** — in the Highlands, three hours north of London, the finest institution on the continent.

> **PROFESSOR WREN:** "Lord Harding. Your application was three months ago."
>
> **NATHANIEL:** (carefully) "I assumed it had been declined."
>
> **PROFESSOR WREN:** "It was not declined. You were accepted in the spring. Your father asked us to hold the letter until tonight."

Nathaniel turns. Eldgard is watching him with an expression that is almost love.

> **NATHANIEL:** "You asked them to hold it?"
>
> **ELDGARD:** "I wanted to give it to you myself."

The letter is placed in his hands. Real, heavy, sealed in wax.

> **NATHANIEL:** (genuinely happy — for the last time this season) "Can I start tomorrow?"
>
> **PROFESSOR WREN:** (dry) "Terms begin in the autumn. But enthusiasm is noted, Lord Harding."

**What the audience learns — AND SHOULD NOT YET QUESTION:** Eldgard wants his surviving son **three hours north of London**, and he wants him there before winter.

> **Plant:** Eldgard applied on his son's behalf. He is trying to get Nathaniel out of the city before the pact comes due. Nathaniel believes it is a gift.

## Scene 6 — The Red Flower (ACT II CENTERPIECE)

*ACT II — "THE PARTY"*

**STRIKING LOCATION:** Eldgard's private study. After the announcement.

Eldgard shuts his study door. A soft knock. Lambrick enters without waiting. He wears the red flower still.

## Scene 6B — THE UNDERCITY: "The Birthday"

*ACT II — "THE PARTY"*

**STRIKING LOCATION:** A small rented room above a laundry. Warm. Cluttered. Cheap, and theirs.

Out of leathers. Coats off. Two young women with a purse of Vykra coin and one evening off.

Miriam is at a cracked mirror, doing her hair with more care than she does anything else. Alice sprawls on the cot, watching her, eating stolen bread.

> **ALICE:** "You've done your hair three times."
>
> **MIRIAM:** "Twice."
>
> **ALICE:** "Three."
>
> **MIRIAM:** "Then stop watching."
>
> **ALICE:** "I'm going to find out who he is."
>
> **MIRIAM:** "You are not."
>
> **ALICE:** "I'm going to follow you one night and ruin everything."
>
> **MIRIAM:** (smiling at the mirror) "You'd have to leave the house. You hate leaving the house."

Alice laughs. Actually laughs — the only time in the episode. Then Miriam, casually, without looking up:

> **MIRIAM:** "I don't need you to know. Not yet."
>
> **ALICE:** "Why not?"
>
> **MIRIAM:** "Because it isn't real until it's mine."

Beat. Alice's smile goes strange. She looks at her hands.

> **ALICE:** "Do you know what today's date is?"

Miriam stops.

> **MIRIAM:** "...Oh."
>
> **ALICE:** "It's fine."
>
> **MIRIAM:** (turning, stricken) "Oh, shoot — I'm sorry. Happy birthday, dear sister."

Alice doesn't answer. She looks away, and for one unguarded second we see it clearly: **not anger. Yearning.** She wants what Miriam has — someone to get ready for.

> **MIRIAM:** "I'll make it up to you. I will. Tomorrow —"
>
> **ALICE:** (flat, meaning none of it) "Tomorrow."

Miriam finishes. Takes her coat. Pauses at the door and looks back, and there is something she decides not to say.

> **MIRIAM:** "Lock the door."

She goes.

**ALONE — AND THE SCENE'S REAL LAST BEAT:**

Miriam, in the corridor, stops at a landing mirror. She looks at herself a moment — then puts a hand flat against her stomach. A small, unnoticeable change. Nothing anyone would see.

She is going to tell someone good news tonight.

**Plant:** Miriam is pregnant. She has told no one. She is on her way to tell the father.

## Scene 8 — Jamie's Farewell

*ACT II — "THE PARTY"*

**STRIKING LOCATION:** The punch bowl. Late in the party. The crowd has thinned.

Jamie finds Nathaniel. Two glasses. Side by side, watching the remaining guests dance.

> **JAMIE:** "Do you ever feel like you're waiting for something terrible? And you can't stop it, but you can't look away?"
>
> **NATHANIEL:** "That's called being sober at a party, Jamie."
>
> **JAMIE:** (doesn't smile) "Remember tonight. Remember that I was happy."

Nathaniel looks at him. Jamie's eyes are wet.

> **NATHANIEL:** "Jamie, you're scaring me."
>
> **JAMIE:** "Good. You should be scared. Just... not of me."

He squeezes Nathaniel's shoulder. Holds it a moment too long. Then walks toward the servants' corridor — toward the basement.

Nathaniel watches him go. Tonight, he should have followed.

## Scene 9 — The Scream

*ACT III — "THE DEATH"*

The party continues. Then — a SCREAM from below. Not human. Not entirely.

Nathaniel's glass shatters on the floor. The Phantom Static spikes — a piercing mechanical whine nobody else hears.

He pushes through the crowd. Across the room, Nixon sees his face, puts down his glass and follows. Down the servants' corridor.

## Scene 10 — The Door

*ACT III — "THE DEATH"*

The reinforced redwood door — three inches thick, iron-banded — has been TORN OPEN FROM THE INSIDE. Splinters the size of daggers. Iron bands twisted like paper.

Movement beyond. A figure — human enough, but *wrong*. Too fluid. Edges that blur. A Chapel assassin wreathed in shadow trails.

It rushes past Nathaniel. NIXON arrives a half-step behind him, sabre out, and swings at a shape that is no longer where it looks. The blade cuts shadow. The figure goes through the window. Glass shattering. Gone.

Nixon stares at the blade. A captain who has seen a war, and he has never seen anything move like that.

> ✏ **EP 1 PASS (mine):** Nixon follows Nathaniel down. He and Nathaniel now share the worst ten minutes of their lives before Act II of the season starts.

## Scene 11 — The Body

*ACT III — "THE DEATH"*

**STRIKING LOCATION:** The basement lab. Charcoal symbols on the walls. Brass instruments scattered. A shattered terrarium — glass twisted as if by immense internal pressure.

And Jamie.

On the floor. Green light still fading from his body. His hand reaches toward the shattered terrarium — toward the empty space where something used to be imprisoned.

He freed it. And he died for it.

> **NATHANIEL:** "JAMIE!"

He falls to his knees. Holds his brother. Jamie is already gone. His face is peaceful — the face of a man who chose this.

## Scene 12 — The Cover-Up

*ACT III — "THE DEATH"*

BYRON pushes through the gathering crowd above.

> **BYRON:** "Everyone out. NOW."

He finds Josephine. Low, urgent:

> **BYRON:** "Get Eldgard. Keep the guests in the ballroom. This cannot leave this house."

Byron reaches the servants' stair. Nixon is already there, sabre still half drawn, his coat gone. He has laid it over Jamie.

> **NIXON:** "Byron. What was that? It went through a window like water."
>
> **BYRON:** "A burglar, Captain. Hold the stair. No one comes down."

Nixon looks at the torn door, then at Byron's hands. He does not believe it. He goes, because Nathaniel is on his knees in there and someone has to keep the house out.

> ✏ **EP 1 PASS (mine):** Nixon is now a second witness who is told a lie in this scene. It gives Nathaniel's Episode 2 request for help ("pull the file") a reason to land on him.

Byron goes on down. Sees Nathaniel holding Jamie, the officer's coat over the body. Sees the shattered terrarium. His scarred hands flex.

> **BYRON:** (to Nathaniel, gentle) "Come away, sir. Come away now."
>
> **NATHANIEL:** "What happened to him? What was he doing down here?"
>
> **BYRON:** "Not here. Not now."

Nathaniel looks up at Byron. The butler's face is controlled devastation.

> **NATHANIEL:** "You knew what was down here."
>
> **BYRON:** (long pause) "I knew what he was trying to do. I couldn't stop him."

**STRIKING IMAGE:** Nathaniel on his knees in the dark. His brother's blood on his white shirt. The handkerchief in his pocket already stained red from his own nosebleed. Two kinds of Harding blood, mingling on silk.

## TAG — "The Bridge"

*TAG*

> **Purpose:** The two stories collide. The audience has spent the episode inside Miriam's evening — they know who she is getting ready for before she does.

**CROSS-CUT — TWO PEOPLE WALKING TO THE SAME PLACE:**

**UPSTAIRS:** EDMUND LAMBRICK arrives late at the party. Collar askew. One button missing from his coat. His hands are damp — recently washed. He takes a glass of champagne from a passing tray, drinks it in one pull. His eyes are somewhere else entirely.

> **TAMARA:** "You look terrible."
>
> **EDMUND:** (too quickly) "I'm fine. Just the cold."

He walks away. Nobody notices.

**DOWNSTAIRS — BLACKWOOD BRIDGE.** Night. Fog thick enough to chew.

MIRIAM BOUNDS stands at the railing. Dressed for an evening she never got to. Poisoned hours ago. Barely able to stand. She is waiting for a man who is not coming, and she is only now understanding that.

**THE LINE THAT MAKES IT LAND:** She has been trying, all night, to reach the one person she wanted to tell. She never gets to. **She dies with the news still in her.**

She pulls a letter from her pocket. Reads it one last time. *"I'm sorry, Alice."*

She climbs the railing. She doesn't hesitate. She jumps.

She hits the water. The water *boils*. Shadows swarm up from the depths. They pour into her eyes, her mouth. Something enters her. Something that was just freed from a shattered terrarium across the city.

**SMASH CUT TO BLACK.**

**What the audience knows that Alice does not:** the mystery man at the party is the man who killed her sister. **They will spend the next two episodes watching Alice not know it.**

## Cold Open — 10 Years Ago

A child's bedroom. Young Nathaniel (10) under blankets. Green light seeps under the door.

**Something enters.** Wrong angles. Too many limbs. A sound like singing and screaming at once.

The boy's eyes meet something in the dark. A face that isn't a face. It tilts its head. Curious.

Then — it's gone. The door closes. The light fades.

Nathaniel screams. No one comes.

**Plant:** He'll see that face again. **PAYOFF IN THIS EPISODE** — Act III, in a police ward, on a girl in a towel. Second payoff in Episode 7 ("I came through the wrong door").

## Scene 1 — A-Story: "The Mourning"

*ACT I — "THE HOUSE CLOSES RANKS"*

**STRIKING LOCATION:** Harding Family Chapel. Rain. Black umbrellas. The entire aristocratic establishment in attendance.

**THE PROTESTS:** Outside the gates, angry working-class citizens protest the ongoing power blackouts and freezing homes. The Royal Guard forms a perimeter wall holding them back as carriages roll past. The Hardings and aristocracy walk past the crowd into the chapel, insulated from the misery. **Fifty seconds of a freezing crowd behind a line of bayonets — this is the season's politics in one image. Do not cut it.**

**THE DUKE** arrives in full state. Black armband. He delivers a brief, moving tribute to Jamie Harding. The audience sees a dignified governor — the Crown's man in London, and every inch of it. **Nixon** stands at attention beside the Duke's carriage — Captain of the Royal Guards in full dress uniform, sword at his hip.

> **DUKE:** "Jamie Harding was the best of us. A young man of conviction, of promise. His loss diminishes us all."

**THE TELL:** When the Duke approaches Eldgard privately afterward, his grip on Eldgard's hand lingers a beat too long. His eyes flick to the crowd — checking who's watching.

> **DUKE:** "Anything you need, Eldgard. Anything at all."
>
> **ELDGARD:** (quiet) "I know."

**What the audience feels:** Something transactional beneath the grief.

**EDMUND AT THE FUNERAL:** Edmund Lambrick stands at the back. Pale. A fresh suit — but his cufflinks don't match. He didn't sleep. When the eulogy mentions Jamie's "bright future," Edmund's hand tightens around his glass. He wasn't close to Jamie. He's not mourning Jamie. He's thinking about the bridge.

**Plant:** The mismatched cufflinks. **PAYOFF IN SCENE 2** — Tamara notices them.

## Scene 2 — C-Story: Tamara & Edmund — "The Heir"

*ACT I — "THE HOUSE CLOSES RANKS"*

> **Runtime: ~2 minutes. Texture beat, not an act turn.**

**STRIKING LOCATION:** The Lambrick carriage, leaving the funeral. Rain on the roof.

TAMARA and EDMUND sit opposite each other. Edmund is already bored.

> **TAMARA:** "You were at the back the whole service. You didn't speak to the family once."
>
> **EDMUND:** "They were busy being tragic. There's a race at three — I've got money on Black Sovereign."
>
> **TAMARA:** "The Hardings are weakening. Father's plan depends on us stepping up when they fall —"
>
> **EDMUND:** "Then let them fall and we'll step. There's a bachelor ball at the Winthrop house on Thursday. Half the eligible daughters in the city will be there."

Beat. Tamara looks at him properly for the first time.

> **TAMARA:** "Your cufflinks don't match."

EDMUND glances down, and for a quarter-second something crosses his face — the mask slipping. Then the grin is back.

> **EDMUND:** "Dressed in the dark. You know how it is."

He laughs a half-second too long. **Tamara doesn't.**

**THE REAL BEAT — AND THE SCENE'S ACTUAL PURPOSE:** Tamara leaves the carriage first. On the steps, alone in the rain, her composure drops. She is more competent, more cunning, and more useful than her half-brother — and her father will hand him the house anyway, because Edmund is a son and she is a daughter.

She doesn't say it. She just looks back at the carriage once.
