# The Green Gallows — Season One
## The Sisters Cut

*Rev. 3's season, with the sisters' storyline from the original draft (the Season Tracker) kept whole.*

The published longread and the Season Tracker are untouched. This is a third document. Everything about the sisters (Alice, Miriam, the thing wearing Miriam, their father, and the man who killed her) comes from the original draft, beat for beat, with its lines intact. Everything else is rev. 3's spine: the gallowglass, the chain under the Works, the Tithe, the countdown to first snow. Where the two drafts had to be joined, I built a seam and marked it.

**Key**
- ◆ — a sisters scene from the original draft, preserved
- ⟡ — a seam, built so the two drafts fit
- Unmarked prose is rev. 3, condensed.

## The sisters

**ALICE BOUNDS, 21.** Small, coiled, dark hair chopped short with a knife and pinned flat. Knuckles always bruised. A Vykra blade since fifteen, the natural kind who goes through a room like weather. She counts guards so her sister doesn't have to. She watches every door. She hates leaving the house.

**MIRIAM BOUNDS, 23.** Taller, softer-faced, the patient one with the plan. Does her hair with more care than she does anything else. Signed her sister into the Vykra as collateral at seventeen and has spent six years trying to make that untrue. Pregnant, and has told no one.

**THE LOST ONE / MIRA.** Morgana's daughter, wearing Miriam's body. She has Miriam's memories the way you're given a house you didn't build: rooms you can walk through, cupboards you've never opened. She doesn't lie, and she isn't sure. Green shows under the brown of her eyes when she's frightened.

**TOBIAS BOUNDS.** Their father. A vat-hand at the Harding Works until the glass got into his blood: tremors, rages, the belt. He sold his daughters' labour for a fee. The Works reassigned him to Kellow Ridge, the Harding pits out on the eastern marsh, on a page Eldgard signed.

**EDMUND LAMBRICK, 20.** Lambrick's bastard son. Handsome in a way that makes dinner guests laugh; up close, something else. Cleans his nails with a penknife. Collar askew, a button missing and damp hands on the night Miriam dies.

**MOTHER GRISE.** Head of the Vykra. A woman with a clerk's hands who counts coin onto tables. Years ago, the recruiter who told a fifteen-year-old she had wolf's eyes.

**THE STREET SURGEON.** Works a cellar clinic under Gin Lane. Used to factory amputations, not miracles. He sees what nobody wants seen.

## Episode 1 — "The Engagement" · 49 Days to First Snow

**Around them.** 1489: Alistair Harding cuts the wrist of the thing in the pit and the first gallowglass lamp lights green; his boy holds the lantern; a tiny girl made of frost slips out and runs into the dark. 1889: London burns Harding green. Nathaniel's gauntlet puts him in the coal bin, and Byron picks soot out of his hair. Eldgard tells Jamie his preparation begins tonight; Jamie leaves his watch wound on the desk. Nixon is pinned captain: "My family looks after its own." At the ball, Nathaniel and Tamara are engaged and agree to be very polite to each other for forty years. "Toys after supper." "You're the son he actually wanted, Nix." The lights go out across London.

**The sisters.**

1. ◆ **The Job.** A rain-slicked roofline in the Narrows. Two figures cross it without a sound. Vykra colours under their coats; a house that sells girls, and a patron who wants it closed.
   > ALICE: "Twelve guards. Four on the door, two on the stair."
   > MIRIAM: "And?"
   > ALICE: "And nothing. I'm counting so you don't have to."
   > MIRIAM: "You always count. You never wait." … "Fast. Quiet. Home before the bells."
   > ALICE: "You have somewhere to be."

   Miriam doesn't answer. Alice notices. Alice always notices.

2. ◆ **The Cargo.** A lamplit storeroom: crates that aren't crates, and a line of girls roped at the wrist. Kestrel is selling. Ajax Fink is buying, with a pleasant face and cold eyes, counting heads like coin. "Then twelve reach the brothels and two don't reach anywhere." Above the grate, Miriam has stopped counting exits. "Miriam. We're here for the ledger." "I know what we're here for."

3. ◆ **The Takedown.** Six seconds per man. Alice goes through the room like weather. Miriam puts a hand flat on Kestrel's table ("I know exactly what they are. That's why I'm here."), and it is not a fight. It is punctuation. Ajax walks out the back with the ledger's first pages and turns at the door to look straight at Miriam. He files her face and smiles. "Tell your patron the house is closed."

4. ◆ **The Report.** Grise counts a purse onto the table, heavier than the job. "The patron should know a man walked out with the first pages. Lambrick's man." *The Vykra protect the Lanes' children. That is how they recruit, and it is not why they were founded.*

5. ◆ **The Birthday.** A rented room over a laundry, warm and cheap and theirs. Miriam is at a cracked mirror doing her hair. Alice sprawls on the cot eating stolen bread.
   > ALICE: "You've done your hair three times." / MIRIAM: "Twice." / ALICE: "Three."
   > ALICE: "I'm going to follow you one night and ruin everything."
   > MIRIAM: "You'd have to leave the house. You hate leaving the house."

   Alice actually laughs, the only time this episode. Then: "I don't need you to know. Not yet." "Why not?" "Because it isn't real until it's mine." Alice's smile goes strange. "Do you know what today's date is?" Miriam stops. "…Oh." "It's fine." "Oh, shoot — I'm sorry. Happy birthday, dear sister." For one unguarded second we see it: not anger, but yearning. Alice wants what Miriam has, someone to get ready for. "Tomorrow." Miriam pauses at the door; there's something she decides not to say. "Lock the door." On the landing, alone, she stops at a mirror and puts a hand flat on her stomach.

6. ⟡ **The door she doesn't lock.** Alice sits with the latch in her hand for a full minute. Then she takes her coat. She tails her sister through the fog toward Mayfair, loses her where the carriages choke the street outside Harding House, and finds a lamp-girl's apron on a hook by the service door. She goes in looking for whoever Miriam did her hair for.

7. **The ballroom, in the dark.** London goes black. Nathaniel hears a woman crying inside the walls. One match flares: the lamp-girl, cupping it. "Do you hear that?" "Hear what, sir?" The match burns down to her fingers and she doesn't flinch. She lifts the schematic of his gauntlet from his coat on her way out. *She came to find a man. She found the wrong one.*

8. ⟡ **The core.** Jamie goes down the thirteen steps with a glass case under his arm: the little green turtle from his mother's drawing room. He sets it at the lock and cuts at the iron with a crystal blade until his hand bleeds into it. Nixon's squad, the strobe, *KRAK*. The case shatters. Something small and cold goes down the blood channels into the dark. Nixon pulls off the mask. Eldgard: "Go, Captain. Wait at the gate. Speak to no one."

9. ◆ **TAG — The Bridge.** Upstairs, Edmund Lambrick arrives late. Collar askew, one button missing, hands damp, recently washed. He drinks a glass of champagne in one pull. TAMARA: "You look terrible." EDMUND: (too quickly) "I'm fine. Just the cold." Nobody notices.

   Blackwood Bridge, fog thick enough to chew. Miriam stands at the railing, dressed for an evening she never got to, poisoned hours ago and barely able to stand. She is waiting for a man who isn't coming, and is only now understanding it. She came out tonight to tell one person good news. She never gets to. She takes a letter from her pocket and reads it one last time. "I'm sorry, Alice." She climbs the railing. She doesn't hesitate.

   She hits the water where the buried Tyburn spills out under the arch. The water boils green. Something that was just freed across the city pours into her eyes and her mouth.

   SMASH CUT TO BLACK.

   *The audience knows the man at the party killed her sister. Alice doesn't, and they'll watch her not know it for weeks.*

## Episode 2 — "The Wake" · 47 Days to First Snow

**Around them.** At Jamie's funeral, Nixon carries a coffin that's too heavy and too still. Nathaniel breaks down halfway through the eulogy. On the wall, a brass: *Ottoline Harding, 1820–1839, Died Abroad.* Nathaniel finds a Lantern Guard cartridge at the scene and hands it to Nixon: "Find me the one who was down there." Nixon is now investigating himself. That night Alice and Nathaniel end up hiding chest to chest in Jamie's wardrobe while Eldgard sits on his dead son's bed and winds his watch; they find the diagram of the chain and the thirteen steps. At the window she gives him one true thing: "Bounds."

**The sisters.**

1. ◆ **The Ward.** The central police ward, green lamps, wet stone. Felix Hawke, coat still wet from the bridge. On a bench at the end sits a girl in a blanket, fished out of the river alive, and nobody knows how. Nathaniel, there to shout about his brother's file, hears the crying from the walls of his house coming from her. He goes grey and leaves. In the doorway he nearly bowls over a woman who ran the whole way. He doesn't register her. She registers him: the man with the match. Alice looks at the girl on the bench and says the words. "That's my sister." She takes her home.

2. ◆ **The Doorway Test.** No dialogue. Alice settles her into the cot and gets nothing back, not grief and not anger: the way someone doesn't answer a language they don't speak yet. A neighbour's door slams on the landing. Alice flinches, as she always has. The girl on the cot doesn't move. All their lives, the real Miriam would have stepped between Alice and any sudden sound. Alice notices the gap before she understands it.

3. ◆ **The Ring.** Josephine alone, her wedding ring on the table, the pale band on her finger. Grise sits. "The Harding boy. Your own son." "He isn't digging. He's grieving. But grief makes people thorough. And thorough people find things." "And if he finds something he shouldn't?" "Then he'll have found it too late." She puts the ring back on. *Never connect this scene to the next one on the page.*

4. ◆ **The Commission.** A knock at the tenement. Folded paper, no crest: shadow an aristocrat, Nathaniel Harding; report what he finds; if he comes too close to something dangerous, redirect him. "Who's the patron?" "Need to know." Alice looks at the name, then at the closed door where her sister isn't sleeping. She takes it.

5. ◆ **The Surgeon.** The cellar clinic under Gin Lane: dripping pipes, tables built for amputations. The surgeon checks pulse and eyes, then stops and looks at Alice over the girl's head. "When did she lose it?" "Lose what?" He wipes his hands and doesn't answer at once, which is the worst thing he does all season. "She was carrying. Two months, perhaps three. The fall, or whatever she took before it — either would have done it. Both together, there was never a chance." "You're wrong." "I'm not wrong. I'm sorry. I'm not wrong."

   Alice kneels and takes her sister's face in both hands. "Who." "A nobleman." "Which one." The girl searches, and finds only this: "From the party. At the Hardings'." Alice, barely audible: "You said it wasn't real yet."

   *She came to find out what was wrong with her sister. She leaves knowing Miriam had a whole life she was never told about, and chose to keep it from her.*

6. **Felix at the door.** Felix calls at the tenement while Alice is out. The girl opens the door, and frost blooms across the frame under her hand. He leaves with a frozen palm and a question he writes down.

7. **Deep under the Works**, something nine feet tall lifts its head for the first time in four hundred years. "My daughter is here."

## Episode 3 — "Lanterns" · 42 Days to First Snow

**Around them.** Eldgard gives Nathaniel the job he's wanted all his life: re-equip the Guard for the Frost Killer hunt. In the corridor he gives Nixon a different order: find the frost girl, bring her to the Works alive, tell no one. Alice reads the sweep roster upside down while Nathaniel shows off his boosters into the coal bin again. Felix corners Nixon: there was a bullet hole in Jamie's coat, and Jamie visited Newgate prisoner 1417 every Sunday for two years. Tamara cracks her father's cipher safe, and Ajax eats an apple on the dark stair. "Evening, miss."

**The sisters.**

1. ◆ **Cold open: Eleven Years Ago.** A labour broker's office: a ledger, a bell, *the improvement of the labouring classes* on the letterhead. Their father signs without reading, watching a clerk count coin. Miriam, 12, stands between the desk and her sister; Alice, 10, stands behind her. "They'll feed them. That's more than I can do." "You said we were going to see the sea." He takes the coin and goes. Miriam's hand is flat against the closing door; Alice's hand is on Miriam's sleeve.

   *Five years later.* A labour-house dormitory, frost on the inside of the glass. Alice is asleep with bruised knuckles. A Vykra recruiter waits at the door. "One girl. The older one." "She'll follow me." "Then she'll be your collateral. That's how this works." "Everyone says that. Nobody says what it means." "It means she is how we know you'll come back." Miriam signs. Her hand shakes. She crosses the room and kisses her sleeping sister's forehead, the last free thing she does.

2. ◆ **Most Of It.** Grey light, a basin, a girl learning what a face is for. A day and a half of waiting quiet, then: "Alice." It's Miriam's voice, and Alice's hand goes still on the cup. "I'm sorry. I — I have most of it. I think I have most of it." "You're my sister. We live together. I know that." (a pause that costs her) "I don't know what we did yesterday." "Nothing. You slept." "Yes. Then I know that." *She isn't lying. She is repeating the sentence she's been told is true and hoping the rest arrives.*

3. ◆ **The Inspection.** Four minutes' warning of a Vykra welfare check. "She'll ask what happened. You fell. You remember the water. You don't remember anything else." "I remember the water." "Good. That's the whole script." "What if I say the wrong—" "Then they decommission you." The agent is kind, which is worse. The girl describes the cold of the water like a scientist and calls Alice *the* sister. The room goes quiet. "The sister." Then she reaches for something she doesn't understand: (flat, bored, exactly Miriam) "Ask me again in a fortnight and I'll be insufferable about it." The agent laughs. "There she is. You had us worried, Bounds." The door closes. Alice listens to the footsteps go down two flights, then sits down on the floor, which is not something she does. "Was that right?" "That was perfect." *The first time she is truly Miriam, she is impersonating her.*

4. ◆ **The Terms.** A tea stall under a broken awning. Nathaniel has worked out that the lamp-girl knows the Lanes, though not that she's paid to watch him. "I can't get into the rooms where Jamie's death is being managed. You can't get into the rooms where my brother's friends drink." She names a rate meant to end the conversation. He pays it without haggling, which insults her more than an argument would. "You're a tourist in your own grief." "My brother is dead and it's the only thing I've been able to think about. I'll take whatever gets me into that room." They write the terms on the back of a betting slip: she walks the Lanes for him, and he takes "Miss Bounds, factory-safety assessor" into society on his arm. *She needs the rooms where a nobleman from the Hardings' party drinks. She doesn't say so. Both are lying about why they want the door.* She takes his hand. He's the first person she's touched on purpose since the bridge.

5. ⟡ **The market.** On the night of the sweep, the girl slips out alone, drawn by a hundred lanterns singing at a Christmas crystal market. She reaches toward them and every crystal flares. Leapers launch across the stalls, flash-bursts strobe the crowd, and she screams. Every crystal in the market shatters. Four people die. Nixon corners her in a bell tower; she reaches for Cole and freezes him from the hand up. Nixon carries Cole to a furnace. She runs. Alice is on the other side of the city with a Harding.

6. ◆ **The Threshold.** Alice wakes to an empty cot and runs into the fog. St Bride's, the Harding church, past midnight. A barefoot girl knocks. A young priest, tired from eleven years of night duty, opens the door: "You're bleeding, child. Come inside." She asks: "I'm a sinner. Please — convert me." He gives her tea, a blanket and gentle questions. And the building notices her: every green lamp in the nave leans toward her, the organ pipes hum, and somewhere below, something that has slept four hundred years turns over. "You have to go." "You said I could—" "I'm sorry. I'm sorry. You have to go." Alice finds her on the steps holding a blanket she was given and then sent away with. "What did you do?" "I asked to come in." (a beat) "They said no." "Good. Stay away from this place."

   *She killed four people tonight, went looking for forgiveness, and doesn't know yet what she is.*

## Episode 4 — "The Hearing" · 38 Days to First Snow

**Around them.** The Crown hearing on the Harding Charter is in three days, and Felix Hawke is on the witness list. Nathaniel and Alice ambush Ajax at the coal docks, the Black Gauntlet hitting like a steam hammer, and hang him off a crane over the river. "Your brother paid me. Lambrick paid me too, but your brother paid double." Nathaniel lets go. Nixon visits prisoner 1417, old Silas Marsh, who sings him a rhyme about apples and thirteen steps. At the hearing a veiled woman brings Felix a glass of water; he dies on the stand with frost on his lips. Nixon burns his rifle's page from the discharge log; Cole copies it from memory. Josephine washes frost-dust from her fingertips, and Byron is in the mirror.

**The sisters.**

1. ◆ **Cold open: Miriam's Last Day.** Miriam walks the factory streets, hollow-eyed, carrying a weight that doesn't show. She writes: *"Alice. I know you think you failed me. You didn't. The world failed both of us. But I can't stay here anymore. Not after what happened. Not after what I let him do…"* She seals it, puts it under her pillow, and walks toward the bridge.

2. ◆ **The Wrong Memories.** She's making their mother's recipe, the one Miriam hated. She hums songs with the wrong words. The Garden Test: "Remember when we snuck into the factory owner's garden?" "Of course. The roses were beautiful." (beat) "We never did that." "I… I must have imagined it."

3. ⟡ **The papers.** On the table, a news-sheet: FROST KILLER — FOUR DEAD AT THE CRYSTAL MARKET. *A dark-haired girl, barefoot.* Alice reads it twice, folds it, and puts it in the stove.

4. ◆⟡ **The Childhood Flashback** *(reconciled with the Ep 3 cold open).* The morning after the forehead kiss. Alice, 15, stands at the door of the Vykra training house. She packed nothing; she has nothing to pack. "The offer was for one." "Then I'll make you buy me too." The recruiter (Grise, younger) studies her. "You have wolf's eyes, girl." Miriam, from the stair: "Don't. Don't do this." Alice walks in. *Miriam signed her in as collateral to keep her safe. Alice made herself a blade instead, and Miriam watched what her sister became.*

5. ◆ **The Almost-Truth.** "Alice… do you ever feel like you're not who you're supposed to be?" "What do you mean?" "Sometimes I have dreams. And I'm not me. I'm something else. Something cold and alone." "You're tired. You need sleep."

6. ◆ **The First Real Conversation.** One candle. "You don't sleep either." "I don't trust sleep. Things happen when you're not watching." … "I have memories. But they feel… borrowed. Like I'm wearing someone else's clothes." Something cracks behind Alice's eyes. "Then we'll figure out who you are. Together." The girl reaches for her hand. Alice lets her take it.

7. ◆ **The Wrong Side of the River.** Nathaniel follows Alice into the street where she grew up. A woman who once shared bread with her spits near his shoe: "Got yourself a gentleman, Alice. Hope he pays better than the last one." A man who knew their father calls her a collaborator. Nathaniel stands there and takes it, which she doesn't expect. The neighbours mention that Miriam came through three weeks before she died, asking where their father had gone. She said she had news. Alice hears *news* and reads it as Miriam going back to the man who hurt them.

8. ⟡ **The crane.** Upside down over the Thames, Ajax looks past Nathaniel at Alice. "I know your face. Your sister's, anyway. She's meant to be in the river."

9. ◆ **The Blackmail.** A tea room on the edge of the Narrows. Josephine intercepts "Miriam" after a shift at the laundry. "My agents tell me Alice is distracted by her sister's return. You will leave the city. I've arranged passage. Do not make me insist." The girl looks at her with an ancient, unblinking stillness. "I'm not leaving Alice." "I can have you removed." The temperature drops. "You think you know all the secrets in your house, Lady Harding. But you missed one. The day the latch was left undone. The little green turtle in your drawing room." Josephine freezes. "You were with the young solicitor. Your husband's solicitor. On the rug. You told him you loved him, but you couldn't leave." … "I won't tell Alice. And I won't tell your husband. Because I don't care. But you will leave Alice and me alone." *Josephine now knows exactly what lives in her watcher's flat. She waits.*

10. ◆ **The Blade.** Alice stands in the doorway over her sleeping sister with a dagger at her side. For a long moment we don't know. "Alice…?" She puts it away and closes the door.

11. ◆ **The Decision.** Dawn. The dagger on the table. (V.O.) "If she's a monster, she doesn't know it. If she's my sister, she's broken. Either way — I need to know for certain." The dagger goes in her belt. "I'll find the truth. And then I'll decide."

## Episode 5 — "Lantern Night" · 33 Days to First Snow

**Around them.** 1489: the first heist on the chain, and three thousand Hargraves hanged on Harding timber. Eldgard brings Nathaniel into the glass vats, and Byron gives him the Guard key Jamie copied: "If I don't come up, give it to Nat." On Lantern Night four plans converge on the Works. Nathaniel's crew goes up the frozen outfall. In the core they see Morgana whole: nine feet of ice and green fire, hooks through her shoulders, singing the Weeping. Nathaniel touches the chain and every lamp in London surges on his blood. Eldgard steps out of the dark and names the Tithe: every fifty years, at first snow, a Harding walks down willingly. "Now it's you." The door seals. Behind the glass, Nixon mouths the oath. Nathaniel stays. "Thirty-three days. Help me find another way." He chooses his father.

**The sisters.**

1. ◆ **The Architect.** Alice takes her back to the surgeon. He checks the pulse, looks into the eyes, and physically recoils. "This isn't sickness. This is engineering. I've only felt this cold once, and it came from the Harding estate. You don't need a doctor. You need an architect. Or an exorcist." In the waiting room a vat-hand passes with crystal dust on his coat. The girl flinches; she has never been afraid of anything, and now she's shaking and doesn't know why. *Miriam's body remembers the belt, and the mine smell that came home on their father's clothes. It's the first time her fear isn't her own.*

2. ⟡ **Why Alice goes up the drain.** Not for Morgana, but for the architect. She goes into the Works with Nathaniel's crew to find out what the Hardings built.

3. ⟡ **"You smell of my daughter."** In the core, Morgana turns her vast head toward the Bounds girl on the gantry and breathes in. "You smell of my daughter." Alice: "I don't know your daughter." On the way out, through the Works office, she takes a vat-hands' roster because it says *Gin Lane* on the spine.

4. ⟡ **Lantern Night, from Gin Lane.** In the core, Morgana screams. Across London, in a tenement room, a girl sitting on a cot screams back. Every lamp in the city blazes white for one heartbeat and bursts. Glass rains on the festival crowds, and people die against the railings on Westminster Bridge.

   Alice comes home through streets of broken glass. Every window on the landing is blown in. Theirs is blown *out*. Her sister is sitting in the glass, unhurt, a thread of blood running from her nose. "Were you hurt?" "No." Alice files it.

5. ◆ **The Shipyard Lie.** The next morning, Alice writes her Vykra report on Nathaniel. She leaves out the core, the chain, and what his father said, and writes *no contact of interest*. It's the first lie she has ever told the people who hold her sister. She stands at the drop a second too long.

## Episode 6 — "Coal" · 28 Days to First Snow

**Around them.** Thirty years ago, young Byron catches a Vykra girl sent to kill Eldgard and lets her go; a year later she marries him. Half the lamps in London are broken, the coal carts say LAMBRICK, and a child falls asleep in a Gin Lane doorway and doesn't wake. Eldgard and Nathaniel design the Heartglass: "We'll need something of her blood to tune it." Byron, in fencing whites, disarms Nathaniel and Nixon together: "You're still thinking like gentlemen. Gentlemen die first." Tamara overhears her father's lunacy plan for her, and learns of the charges in the Tyburn conduit. At a frozen canal, Alice's glove falls open and Nathaniel's handkerchief drops into the frost; he kisses her. Byron confronts Josephine: "I saw your hands." In the gasworks, Byron carries his old green Lantern for the first time in thirty years and pulls Nathaniel out from under a beam. ⟡ He lives, burned down one side. The Crown seizes the Works, and Lambrick takes a seat on its board.

**The sisters.**

1. ◆ **On Her Behalf.** Hours before dawn; Alice is bandaged from the gasworks. The girl has been quiet in the waiting way, like someone who's read the whole book and is deciding whether to say what's in it. "I know what she did." "Who." "Miriam. I have all of it now. Not the way you have it. The way you have a room — you can walk into any part of it, but you didn't build it." "She signed the paper." "I know." "She was twelve." "I know." "She watched them take you." Then she says his name: **Tobias.** And she describes one night: the belt, the stair, and Miriam taking three blows meant for Alice. Alice didn't know. She has believed for eleven years that Miriam left her to it.
   > LOST ONE: "She wants you to forgive her for not being able to stop him. For not being big enough. For not being there. For signing."

## Episode 7 — "The Girl Who Isn't" · 21 Days to First Snow

## Episode 8 — "The Wedding" · 14 Days to First Snow

**Around them.** Sixteen years ago, Nathaniel, five, follows a lullaby down forbidden stairs and finds a gentle woman of ice who sings his name; his father swings a lantern before his eyes. "You had a dream." Nixon opens the folder: *Hargrave, N. Cadet line of Harding. Reserve. Blood suitable.* In the chapel, Tamara says: "Say one word and I'll walk out of this church with you." He can't.

**The sisters.**

1. ◆ **Byron's Breakfast.** The glasshouse workshop on the day before the wedding: bread, eggs, a window cracked to let the steam out. This is the only undisturbed happy scene in the season. She calls him "Lord Harding" in a flat voice and he pretends to be offended; they bicker over who stole whose coat. Byron arrives with food he says was surplus and pours the tea as if he'd seen nothing. At the stove, quietly, he tells her: "I've known for some time who you report to. He doesn't. I'd ask that he hear it from you, and soon." "Today. I'll tell him today." She means it. The story takes the chance away within hours.

2. ◆ **The Promise.** The tenement at dusk, one candle. A lead has come in through the Vykra, and Alice is dressing to go.
   > LOST ONE: "Whatever happens tonight—" / ALICE: "Don't." / LOST ONE: "I need to say this." … "I'm not Miriam. I never was. But the love I feel for you — that's real. That's mine. Not borrowed. Not inherited from her memories. Mine." … "Being your sister, even for a few months, was worth existing for. You made me want to be human. You made me human."

4. ◆ **Byron's Last Stand.** Eight Vykra in black masks surround the tenement with orders to take the creature alive. Green light gathers in her hands. Then Byron comes up the stair, burned down one side from the gasworks: an old man with young eyes. "Not this." "Lady Harding has gone too far." He draws twin blades from a coat he's worn for thirty years. Two men go down, fast and ugly. He's magnificent. But there are eight. A blade finds his back, then another. He looks at her. "Run. 5. ◆⟡ **The Decoy Sacrifice.** She runs through the fog with the Vykra behind her, and feels her mother in every lamp she passes. She could run to Alice. She stops. It names the watcher, and it names who poisoned Felix Hawke.
   > NATHANIEL: "It says my mother is Vykra. It says Vykra put someone on me the week Jamie died. It says you."
   > ALICE: "I didn't know it was her." / NATHANIEL: "But you knew it was someone." / ALICE: "Yes."
   > NATHANIEL: "Was any of it real?"

   She can't answer. *"I don't know where it changed" is the honest answer, and it sounds like the worst possible one.* He takes the letter and Byron's blades. "I'm done being a Harding. I don't know what I'm done being with you."

14. ◆ **The Lost Hour.** An alley three streets away. Alice wakes on the cobbles, gasping, her head spinning as if she's been drugged. There's glittering dust on her cuff. One hour is gone. "No. No, I'm not losing him." She climbs the trellis again. The room has been tidied; there are drag marks, and two sets of boots walking backward. *She believes he got away.*

15. **The core.** In the Heartglass beside her mother, the girl speaks the language of the White. "I've come to take your place." For four hundred years Morgana has wanted the door, and here it is. She screams in grief, not rage, drags herself back onto the hooks, and cracks the glass from her side. "Not her." It begins to snow, two weeks early. **0 DAYS.**

## Episode 9 — "The Snow Confession" · First Snow, Dusk to Midnight

**Around them.** In the bell foundry, forty disgraced Lanterns cook sausages on their gauntlets. "You shot my brother." "Yes." "My father did the rest." "What do you need?" Lambrick orders Tamara to deliver Nathaniel by midnight; she opens his safe one last time and takes the Works master key, and says nothing about what she saw from the window. Nixon's forty come over the Newgate walls on leap-boosters. Old Silas Marsh, released, looks at the snow: "They always count from the first snow." There's a three-way battle in the snow at the Works, then the wolves come. Ajax drives Nathaniel into the ground, and Eldgard steps under the killing blow. Dying, he tells his son he held Jamie's hand on the iron until the crack closed, that Nixon was bred for the stair, and that he could have gone down himself. He presses the wolf-crest ring into Nathaniel's palm. "Don't be me." At the core, Josephine pulls the lever.

**The sisters.**

1. ◆ **The Longest Night.** The Heartglass, snow falling through the vents. She isn't afraid. She's listening. Under the Works, a wall of Hargrave dead turn over in their sleep. Out on the eastern marsh, the Kellow Ridge pits are going cold with no coal, and the newly dead arrive one by one. "I know. I know. I'm sorry." Morgana, patient as geology: "They cannot hear you, child. They are only fuel." "Then I'll talk to them anyway. Someone should." For the first time in four hundred years, her mother looks uncertain.

2. ◆⟡ **I Owe Someone an Hour.** The glasshouse, with snow coming through the broken panes. Alice tells Nathaniel before he can ask. "I lost an hour outside Edmund Lambrick's door. I don't know what I did in it." "You didn't tell me." "I'm telling you now." He's angry, and moved, and takes his time choosing which to say. "Don't do that again without me." After that I'd have done it for nothing." *It's the first secret she's handed over without being cornered.*

3. ⟡ **Wolf's eyes.** Alice calls a challenge by Vykra law: a knife fight among steaming sheets. Grise, who recruited her, is the one across from her. Alice wins and spares her. "You told me I had wolf's eyes." The Vykra girls pick up their knives. "Where?" "Tyburn."

4. **She stays.** When Eldgard goes down under Ajax's blow, Alice has the charge to chase Ajax, and the door to the core in front of her with her sister behind it. She looks at Nathaniel on his knees with his father across his lap. She stays.

## Episode 10 — "The Tithe" · The Last Night, Midnight to Dawn

**Around them.** 1489: "Free me, and pay in light. Keep me, and pay in sons." "Sons." The lever drops and green fire floods the cage; Morgana drags herself back onto the iron. "Not her." Josephine fights Alice, Vykra against Vykra, the mother against the girl she paid. Nixon comes down the stair bleeding: "I'm Harding blood. Take me." Nathaniel: "No one goes in the chain again." Jamie's blade is frozen in the lock; Alice puts her overloading gauntlet against the iron and holds it there while it burns her palm. "They'll hate me for a hundred years." "Us." Blood, ring, blade. A church bell strikes twelve. The chain falls and the green dies street by street, and for the first time since he was five, nothing is crying in the walls. Morgana rises ("A Harding opened it") and goes up through the Works toward Tyburn hill with the White behind her. Nixon kills Ajax. Cole holds Tyburn Road with his last cartridge. In the vault, Tamara leaves her father to his satchels of dying glass, and the wolves come in.

**The sisters.**

1. ◆ **Kellow Ridge.** Alice smashes the cracked Heartglass and drags her out, half turned to crystal. On the stair, through chattering teeth: "There was a man. He smelled like the dust. He said two names at the end." Alice doesn't ask which. She doesn't cry. "Good." Then she sits down in the snow. *She loses the confrontation she spent eleven years building. 2. ◆ **The Choice.** Tyburn hill, the city below, wolves in a ring. The girl sets herself down between her mother and London.

   > LOST ONE: "You could force me. You have the power." / MORGANA: "Yes."
   > LOST ONE: "But you won't. Because you remember when I was small."

5. ◆ **The Shot.** Tamara steps out of the treeline, pistol in hand, straight from the vault where she left her father to the wolves. Her eyes catch the black silk tied around Alice's burned palm. 7. ◆⟡ **What It Costs Mira.** She kneels and presses her hands to the wound. There's a little of the White left in her, the last of it, and she spends it, freezing the blood still under her palms. Stay with me." *The sister who couldn't protect has become the sister who does, in a different body.* Alice, with blood on her lips: "I found him. I finally found him. I didn't even get to feel it…" "It's… fair." Nathaniel: "Then don't end. Stay."

8. ◆ **The Carry.** Nixon crosses the snow and kneels without asking. "On three. Don't drop her." They lift her together. Mira walks beside them with her hands on the wound, down Tyburn hill toward a cellar clinic under Gin Lane and a man used to factory amputations, not miracles. The two men move in step, and neither has said sorry.

**Coda.** London is cold, and candles pass from window to window down Gin Lane. A chisel takes DIED ABROAD off a brass in St Bride's, one letter at a time. Hard cut to the White. Josephine walks beside Morgana and stops. A man stands in the snow with frost in his hair: Jamie, exactly as he went down. Far behind him, a boy of nine holds up a lantern, still burning after four hundred years. Josephine sits down beside her son.

## Still open (carried over from the original)

- **What Edmund says in the hour.** The original draft's version: Miriam came to him that night and told him about the child; he gave her Xyon in wine and called it kindness; she understood what was in the cup, finished it, and walked to the bridge. The colder option is that he simply poisoned her. Ep 10's flash needs one of them.
- **Does Alice live.** The original leaves it on her face at dawn. This cut keeps it open into Season Two.
- **Tamara.** She shot the woman who killed her brother, on the morning she left her father to the wolves, and Nixon is holding her down. Where she wakes up in Season Two is a choice.
- **Josephine and the turtle.** Seam 1 means Josephine lived ten years beside the glass case without knowing it was watching her. If she did know, the Ep 4 blackmail plays differently.
